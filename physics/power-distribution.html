Purpose

What Happens When Things Fail

In aerospace, things WILL fail. The question is: does the mission survive? Power distribution architecture determines single-point failures. Click "Blow Fuse" to simulate failures and see the impact.

The Physics

Redundancy

Single-fault tolerant: survives any ONE failure

Dual-fault tolerant: survives any TWO failures (human-rated)

Power Budget

Total generation > Total consumption + margin (10-30%)

Key Insight: Single-point failure analysis traces every power path. If ONE wire/fuse/connector failure kills the mission, it's a design error. Artifact's redundancy checking automates this analysis.

Historical

Apollo 13 (1970)
Oxygen tank explosion severed primary power bus. Crew survived ONLY because the Lunar Module had an independent power system. Redundancy saved three lives.
