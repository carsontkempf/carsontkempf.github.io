<h1>From Ohm's Law to Orbital Wiring</h1>
<p>Interactive physics simulations built from scratch in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly. Each module teaches the physics and produces a working simulation you can interact with in your browser.</p>

<h2>Fundamentals</h2>
<ul>
  <li><a href="/physics/dc-circuits">Module 1: DC Circuit Fundamentals</a> -- Ohm's Law, KCL, KVL, voltage dividers</li>
  <li><a href="/physics/wire-properties">Module 2: Wire Properties & Voltage Drop</a> -- AWG sizing, temperature derating, MIL-spec</li>
  <li><a href="/physics/rc-dynamics">Module 3: RC/RL Dynamics</a> -- Time constants, transient response, filtering</li>
</ul>

<h2>Systems</h2>
<ul>
  <li><a href="/physics/emi-shielding">Module 4: Signal Integrity & EMI</a> -- Crosstalk, shielding, MIL-STD-461</li>
  <li><a href="/physics/power-distribution">Module 5: Power Distribution</a> -- Bus architecture, redundancy, failure analysis</li>
  <li><a href="/physics/connectors">Module 6: Connectors & Pin-Level Design</a> -- MIL-DTL-38999, pin assignments</li>
  <li><a href="/physics/control-systems">Module 7: Control Systems (PID)</a> -- Feedback, stability, tuning</li>
  <li><a href="/physics/thermal-analysis">Module 8: Thermal Analysis</a> -- I2R heating, derating, harness bundles</li>
</ul>

<h2>Aerospace</h2>
<ul>
  <li><a href="/physics/attitude-dynamics">Module 9: Rotational Dynamics</a> -- Quaternions, reaction wheels, keep-out zones</li>
  <li><a href="/physics/orbital-mechanics">Module 10: Orbital Mechanics</a> -- Kepler, Hohmann transfers, ground tracks</li>
  <li><a href="/physics/evolutionary-routing">Module 11: Evolutionary Optimization</a> -- Genetic algorithms for harness routing</li>
</ul>

<h2>Capstone</h2>
<ul>
  <li><a href="/physics/cubesat-ewis">Module 12: CubeSat EWIS Design</a> -- Complete wiring interconnect system</li>
</ul>
